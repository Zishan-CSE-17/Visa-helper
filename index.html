Where each answer goes

The site looked for the printed labels on the form and picked the empty box next to each one. Check the list and fix any cell. Cell examples: C5 · Sheet2!C5 · dates in separate boxes C5,E5,G5 (year, month, day) · circle options Male=E5;Female=G5. Leave a box empty to skip it.
